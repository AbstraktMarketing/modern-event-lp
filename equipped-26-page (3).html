FAQ

What is MODERN?

MODERN is a two-way customer interaction platform built for heavy equipment dealers. It connects customers, service teams, parts, and your DMS in one workflow so approvals move faster.

Does MODERN replace my DMS?

No. MODERN works alongside your existing DMS or ERP and syncs work order data without writing over your core system.

Which dealers use MODERN?

Construction, agriculture, material handling, mining, sanitation, forestry, and landscape dealers across the US and Canada, including dealers for Bobcat, Vermeer, John Deere, and Kubota.
